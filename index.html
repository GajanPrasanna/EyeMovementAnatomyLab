Extraocular muscle reference

Main actions and cranial nerves
Eye movementMain muscle(s)Cranial nerve
Abduction (eye moves laterally)Lateral rectusCN VI — Abducens
Adduction (eye moves medially)Medial rectusCN III — Oculomotor
Elevation (eye moves upward)Superior rectus and inferior obliqueCN III — Oculomotor
Depression (eye moves downward)Inferior rectus and superior obliqueCN III — Oculomotor; CN IV — Trochlear
Intorsion (top of eye rotates medially)Superior rectus and superior obliqueCN III — Oculomotor; CN IV — Trochlear
Extorsion (top of eye rotates laterally)Inferior rectus and inferior obliqueCN III — Oculomotor

Horizontal gaze is conjugate: one eye abducts while the other adducts. Vertical actions of the recti and obliques vary with eye position; the table gives the main muscle groups for teaching. The live software estimates direction from a single eye and cannot establish nerve function.

Privacy: the application displays a peer-to-peer camera stream and does not intentionally upload or record video. PeerJS uses an online signalling service to establish the connection. Do not use identifiable patient footage without informed consent.
